THE MISSION

Why Recycle?

Saves Energy

Producing goods from recycled materials consumes significantly less energy than manufacturing from raw materials.

Reduces Pollution

Recycling minimizes the need for mining and logging, protecting our air, water, and soil from toxic pollutants.

Protects Wildlife

Less waste means healthier ecosystems for animals and prevents dangerous plastics from entering marine habitats.
